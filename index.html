MEET YOUR TEACHER

15 years of music experience. A clear path for every learner.

I'm Lokesh, a music teacher with 15 years of experience studying and exploring Carnatic and Western music. I am certified by LCM — London College of Music.

Over the years, I've developed simple ways and practical formulas to help students understand musical concepts more easily. My teaching starts with a strong foundation, because consistent practice and clear basics make long-term progress possible.

I encourage every student to practise for at least 15 minutes a day. During my own learning years, I often practised for three to four hours a day, and I bring that respect for steady practice into my teaching.

PIANO COURSE

Build your piano foundations

The main piano course covers the following concepts, progressing according to the student's performance and learning pace.

  • Finger exercises
  • Major scales
  • Minor scales
  • Major chords
  • Minor chords
  • Major families
  • Minor families
  • Chord progressions
  • Chord inversions
  • Relative minors
  • Circle of fifths
